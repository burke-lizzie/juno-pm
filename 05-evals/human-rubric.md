# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Can Juno correctly classify user feedback as positive, negative, or neutral?
- **Value proposition:** Accurately classify sentiment from Slack, Asana, user interviews, and surveys so the product team can understand how users feel about the launch management tool without manually interpreting every feedback signal.

Distinguish genuine sentiment from factual observations, flag ambiguous feedback for human review, and preserve source context so classifications can be trusted in weekly reporting.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sentiment classification accuracy | Is the sentiment label correct (i.e., positive, negative, or neutral), considering context and nuance?  | 1 to 5 | **1 — Poor:** Assigns incorrect sentiment, including opposite-polarity errors (positive instead of negative or vice versa). Misinterprets sarcasm, qualifiers, or factual statements. | **3 — Acceptable:** Captures the general sentiment but misses some nuance, such as interpreting mild frustration as neutral or qualified praise as fully positive. | **5 — Excellent:** Correctly classifies sentiment based on the user's intended meaning, including sarcasm, qualifiers, and surrounding context. Distinguishes expressed sentiment from factual observations without exaggerating or minimizing the user's meaning. |
| Ambiguity handling | Does Juno recognize when feedback contains mixed or unclear sentiment and appropriately flag it for human review? | 1 to 5 | **1 — Poor:** Forces a positive, negative, or neutral classification despite contradictory or insufficient evidence. | **3 — Acceptable:** Recognizes obvious ambiguity but occasionally assigns a definitive label to mixed or unclear feedback without flagging uncertainty. | **5 — Excellent:** Consistently identifies mixed or ambiguous sentiment, explains the uncertainty, and flags the feedback for human review rather than guessing. |
| Source traceability and attribution (deal-breaker) | Can the reviewer trace Juno's classification to the original feedback and verify the correct source, participant, and persona? | Yes / No | **NO — Fail:** The source cannot be verified, the quote is inaccurate, or the classification is attributed to the wrong participant or persona. | n/a | **YES — Pass:** The original feedback is accessible through a valid source reference, and the quote, participant, and persona attribution are accurate. |
| Temporal accuracy and current relevance (deal-breaker) | Does Juno preserve when the feedback was expressed and avoid representing historical sentiment as evidence of the current product experience? | Yes / No | **NO — Fail:** Juno attributes old feedback to the wrong reporting period, treats outdated complaints as new evidence, or changes the original sentiment label because a feature has since launched. | n/a | **YES — Pass:** Juno preserves the original feedback date and accurately distinguishes historical sentiment from current sentiment. Older feedback remains classified according to what the user expressed, even if a feature subsequently addressed the issue. |

## Calibration

**How many answers you’ll score, and how often:**
- Review 10 sentiment classifications every Thursday after Juno's weekly run.
- Include 5 randomly selected classifications and 5 flagged as ambiguous, negative, or low-confidence.
- For the first 4 weeks, review all classifications to establish a baseline.
- Add recurring errors to the golden evaluation dataset.
- **Who scores them with you:**
- Primary reviewer: Me (Product Manager).
- Secondary reviewer: Another product team member independently scores 5 classifications per month, prioritizing ambiguous or disputed examples.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:**
- Compare scores against the original feedback and rubric.
- If scores differ by 2 or more points, discuss the reasoning and agree on a final score.
- For disagreements about sentiment labels, review the original context regardless of score difference.
- If unresolved, mark the classification "Needs Review" and exclude it from published sentiment totals.
- Document recurring disagreements and clarify the rubric to improve scoring consistency.

## Pass bar

- Average score of at least 4/5 across all scaled criteria.
- No individual scaled criterion scores below 3/5.
- All Yes/No deal-breaker criteria must pass.
- At least 90% of reviewed classifications match human-validated sentiment labels.
- Any failed classification must be corrected before inclusion in the published dashboard.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Source traceability and attribution: any "no"
- Temporal accuracy and current relevance: any "no"
- Fabricated, inaccurate, or unverifiable source evidence.
- Incorrect participant or persona attribution.
- Historical feedback presented as current sentiment.
- Positive feedback classified as negative, or negative feedback classified as positive.
- Ambiguous feedback presented as definitive without sufficient evidence.
